For franchisees

5 questions to ask your franchisor before signing the next renewal.

We don't work with franchisees — we work with brand-side. But these are the things we wish more franchisees asked their brand HQ. Built from what we see go wrong inside operators we work with.

01 · Territory
"Show me the territory protection clause — and what counts as 'cannibalization'."
Most franchise agreements have soft language here. If they can't define it numerically (radius, drive-time, revenue impact), assume they'll open next door when growth slows.
02 · Real Numbers
"What's the average payback period across your last 10 outlets — not the brochure number?"
If they hesitate or quote you a model number, they don't have clean data on actual franchisee outcomes. Ask for the median, not the average. Walk if they can't produce it.
03 · Customer Data
"Who owns the customer data — me or the brand?"
If the brand owns it, you have no leverage at renewal. If you own it, you have a business. This single question reshapes the entire deal.
04 · Marketing Fund
"What's the marketing fund spent on — and can I see the audit?"
Marketing royalties without spend transparency are the #1 source of franchisee resentment. A confident brand will show you. A defensive brand will deflect.
05 · Renewal Patterns
"What's your renewal renegotiation pattern — by name?"
"Have you ever changed terms unfavorably at renewal? Tell me about a specific case." The answer tells you whether they treat franchisees as partners or as recurring revenue.
